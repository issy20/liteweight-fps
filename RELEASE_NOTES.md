# liteweight-fps 0.1.0 Preview

This is the first preview of the offline single-player BOT training FPS.

## Included

- Free Training and Tracking Aim practice modes.
- First-person movement, mouse look, sprinting, jumping, and collision with the arena.
- Three original weapons with ADS, recoil, firing, reload, and weapon switching.
- Static, strafing, and chasing BOT targets with configurable population and behavior.
- Local settings for sensitivity, field of view, fullscreen, audio, ammunition, hit-marker strength, and BOT behavior.

## Distribution

- Distributed as source. Build it yourself with the Rust stable toolchain; no prebuilt or signed binaries are provided.
- Build targets: Windows x64, Linux x64, macOS Apple Silicon, and macOS Intel.
- See `README.md` and `DISTRIBUTION.md` for build steps and platform requirements.

## Preview limitations

- Gameplay performance has not been measured on representative low-end hardware. No minimum FPS or GPU support is guaranteed.
- Runtime behavior has not been verified on all supported operating systems. CI compilation and automated tests do not replace device testing.

This preview is for evaluation. Do not treat it as a claim of verified performance or compatibility.
